The Crowe Playwright Framework (CPF) provides teams with a streamlined approach to UI test automation. It comes equipped with built-in support for a range of tools, enhancing its overall functionality and user experience. 

CPF enable us to use [Playwright](https://playwright.dev/) for E2E automation effectively & quickly. CPF doesn’t overwrite the Playwright's methods.
